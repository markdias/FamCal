# FamCal - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 11, 2025**

## 1. Introduction

FamCal ("we," "us," "our," or the "App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information**: Email address, name, and password (used for authentication)
- **Profile Information**: Family member names and relationships
- **Invitation Information**: Email addresses of family members you invite to share calendars
- **User Preferences**: Theme preferences, notification settings, and date range preferences for calendar display

### 2.2 Calendar Data

FamCal accesses your iOS Calendar through Apple's EventKit framework. We collect and store:

- Event titles
- Event dates and times
- Event descriptions and notes
- Event attendees and organizers
- Calendar names and identifiers
- Recurring event information

**Important**: You must explicitly grant FamCal permission to access your calendar through iOS Settings. You can revoke this permission at any time, and FamCal will immediately stop accessing your calendar data.

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information

- **Device Information**: Device model, iOS version, app version
- **Usage Information**: Features used, frequency of use, actions performed within the app
- **Crash and Performance Data**: Error logs and performance metrics to help us improve the app
- **Advertising Identifiers**: For the purpose of delivering personalized ads

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

### 3.1 Primary Uses

- **Family Calendar Sharing**: To sync and display calendar information among family members you've authorized
- **User Authentication**: To verify your identity and maintain your account
- **Communication**: To send you account-related notifications and family invitations
- **Service Improvement**: To analyze usage patterns and improve app functionality
- **Support**: To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support

### 3.2 Advertising

FamCal uses Google Mobile Ads to display personalized advertisements. Google may collect and use:

- Advertising identifiers
- General location information (not precise)
- Device information
- Usage information

To learn more about Google's privacy practices, visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized ads through your iOS Settings:
- Settings > Privacy > Apple Advertising > Limit Ad Tracking

## 4. How We Share Your Information

### 4.1 Shared with Family Members

Calendar information and events you mark as shared are visible to family members you've explicitly invited and who have accepted invitations.

### 4.2 Third-Party Service Providers

We share information with third parties who perform services on our behalf:

- **Supabase**: Cloud infrastructure and database services (https://supabase.io)
- **Google Mobile Ads**: Advertising and analytics services
- **Apple**: EventKit access and device identifiers

### 4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or if we believe in good faith that such disclosure is necessary to:

- Comply with legal obligations
- Protect our legal rights
- Investigate or prevent fraud or security issues
- Protect the safety of users or the public

### 4.4 No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not share your calendar data with advertisers or other commercial entities beyond what is necessary for ad personalization as described above.

## 5. Data Security

FamCal implements administrative, technical, and physical security measures designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

**Security Measures Include**:

- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Encrypted storage of sensitive data
- Secure authentication mechanisms
- Regular security updates
- Access controls limiting employee access to personal data

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Account Data**: Retained while your account is active. Upon account deletion, data is retained for 30 days to allow for recovery, then permanently deleted.
- **Calendar Data**: Synced data is stored while you maintain the connection. If you revoke calendar permissions, we stop collecting new data but may retain previously collected events for up to 90 days before deletion.
- **Logs and Analytics**: Retained for up to 12 months for performance and troubleshooting purposes.
- **Backup Data**: Backups may be retained for longer periods but will be deleted after the account retention period.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

### 7.1 Access and Portability

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and to receive it in a portable format.

### 7.2 Correction and Deletion

You can update your account information directly in the App. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time through the app settings or by contacting us.

### 7.3 Opt-Out Options

- **Calendar Access**: Revoke in iOS Settings > Privacy > Calendar
- **Personalized Ads**: Enable "Limit Ad Tracking" in iOS Settings
- **Notifications**: Disable notifications in App Settings or iOS Settings
- **Data Sharing**: Manage which family members have access through Family Management settings

### 7.4 Jurisdiction-Specific Rights

**For GDPR Compliance (EU Residents)**:
- Right to access your personal data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Right to withdraw consent

**For CCPA Compliance (California Residents)**:
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to know whether personal information is sold or disclosed
- Right to delete personal information collected from consumers
- Right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights

## 8. Children's Privacy

FamCal does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information and terminate the child's account.

Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided information to FamCal should contact us immediately.

Your continued use of the App following the posting of revised Privacy Policy means that you accept and agree to the changes.

## 10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through:

- **In-App Feedback**: Use the feedback feature in App Settings

We will respond to privacy inquiries within 30 days.

## 11. Specific Data Processing Details

### 11.1 Cloud Storage (Supabase)

FamCal uses Supabase, a cloud platform built on PostgreSQL, to store:
- User account credentials
- Family relationship information
- Calendar linking information
- Event metadata

Supabase implements security measures including encryption, access controls, and regular backups. For more information, visit: https://supabase.io/privacy

### 11.2 iOS Calendar Access

When you authorize FamCal to access your calendar:
- FamCal reads events from your device's calendar database
- Only events you explicitly sync are stored on our servers
- You can revoke access at any time without penalty
- Revoking access stops syncing but doesn't affect existing calendar events on your device

### 11.3 Advertising Networks

Google Mobile Ads may collect:
- Device identifiers (IDFA)
- General location (not precise)
- App usage information
- Device specifications

This information is used for interest-based advertising. You can manage your advertising preferences in iOS Settings.
